Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

1. Overview

FocusPaw is a privacy-first focus tracker extension for Google Chrome. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. The most important thing to know: FocusPaw is designed to collect zero personal data and store everything locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally

FocusPaw stores the following data only on your device using Chrome's local storage API:

  • Domain names — the websites you visit (e.g., "github.com")
  • Visit counts — how many times you visit each domain per day and per 5-hour window
  • Limit configurations — the daily and 5-hour visit limits you set for each domain
  • Streak data — your focus streak count
  • Focus scores — your daily focus score (0–100)

2.2 Information NOT Collected

FocusPaw does NOT collect or store:

  • Full URLs or page titles
  • Time spent on each page
  • Page content, text, or media
  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Browser history outside of FocusPaw
  • Any data from Incognito/Private browsing

3. How We Use Your Data

All data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your browsing patterns in the dashboard visualization
  • Enforcing the visit limits you configure for distracting websites
  • Calculating your focus streak and daily focus score
  • Providing you with export options (CSV/JSON) for your own analysis

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This means:

  • No data leaves your computer — FocusPaw never connects to external servers, analytics services, or cloud storage
  • No account required — we don't know who you are
  • No tracking across websites — we only see domain names, not full URLs or page content
  • Your data is encrypted by Chrome — stored using Chrome's built-in storage encryption

5. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Export: Download all your data in CSV or JSON format from Settings
  • Delete individual domains: Right-click any domain in the table and select "Delete"
  • Delete everything: Go to Settings → Data Management → "Reset All Focus Data"

Deleted data cannot be recovered. We recommend exporting your data before deleting.

6. Permissions

FocusPaw requires the following Chrome permissions:

  • tabs — to detect when you navigate to a new domain
  • storage — to save your settings and browsing data locally
  • notifications — to show you limit warnings and focus reminders
  • declarativeNetRequest — to block access to distracting sites when limits are exceeded
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess — required for redirect-blocking to work properly
  • Host permissions (<all_urls>) — optional, only granted when you choose to enforce blocking on specific sites

7. Third-Party Services

FocusPaw does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising.

The extension is open source and available on GitHub for independent audit.

8. Children's Privacy

FocusPaw is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FocusPaw handles your data, you can:

  • Open an issue on our GitHub repository
